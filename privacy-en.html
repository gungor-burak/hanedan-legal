Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 20, 2026 · App: Successor: Ottomans · Publisher: Burak Güngör

This Privacy Policy describes how Successor: Ottomans (the "App"), published by Burak Güngör, handles information when you use it.

Summary

The App is free to download and playable in an ad-supported mode. In the free version, ads are served through Google AdMob, which collects certain data to serve and measure ads, as described below. If you buy the optional Full Version (Premium), no ads are shown and no advertising data is collected; in that case AdMob is never initialized. Game progress is saved locally on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, may also be backed up to your own iCloud account (see Section 4).

1. Advertising and Google AdMob

In the free version, the App shows ads through Google's AdMob service (interstitial ads, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch). To serve, frequency-cap, prevent fraud, and measure ads, AdMob and Google's advertising partners may collect and process certain data. How Google uses this data is described at policies.google.com/privacy and Google AdMob data usage.

2. Data Collected for Ads

In the free (ad-supported) version, Google AdMob and its partners may collect the following data types, used for third-party advertising and analytics/measurement:

Data typeDescription
Coarse locationApproximate (city/region level) location, for ad relevance.
Device identifiersAdvertising identifier (such as IDFA/IDFV) and similar identifiers.
Product interactionInteraction events with ads and the app.
Advertising dataData about ads shown or clicked.
Crash dataCrash information for ad SDK stability.
Performance dataPerformance metrics such as load time.

For Premium (Full Version) users, no ads are shown and AdMob is not initialized, so none of this data is collected for advertising.

3. Consent Management: UMP and ATT

On first eligible launch, the App may present privacy/consent choices via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) (notably in the European Economic Area). On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt is also shown.

  • If you allow it, ads may be personalized to your interests.
  • If you decline, only general (non-personalized) ads are shown and the game plays the same way; gameplay is not restricted.
  • You can reopen your privacy choices from the Settings menu when required.

4. Local Data and iCloud Backup

The App stores your game progress, settings, and purchase status in a sandboxed file on your device. Uninstalling the App removes this local data.

In addition, Successor: Ottomans may use Apple iCloud to back up your game progress so it can be restored after reinstalling the App or moving to another device. This backup uses Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage tied to your own Apple ID; it is stored in your personal iCloud account, which we cannot access. This data is used only for app functionality (restoring your progress) and is never used for tracking, advertising, or any other purpose. If you are not signed in to iCloud, the App simply uses local save and no backup is made.

5. In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time in-app purchase that removes ads and unlocks the full version ("Full Version"). This purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, see, or store your payment information at any point. Apple's privacy policy governs the payment transaction: apple.com/legal/privacy. Apple's StoreKit framework is used to verify purchase status.

6. Third-Party Services

The third-party services the App communicates with are: Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform for ads (free/ad-supported version only), Apple StoreKit for in-app purchases, and Apple iCloud for the game-progress backup (Section 4). These services are operated by their respective providers.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is rated for users aged 13 and over (App Store Age Rating: 13+) and is not directed at children under 13. Ad content is configured not to exceed the app's age rating. If you become aware that a child under 13 is using the App without your knowledge, please contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Effective date" at the top and publish the new version at the same URL. Continued use of the App after a change indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, write to 9er.game@gmail.com.
