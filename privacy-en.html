Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 14, 2026 · App: Dynasty: Ottoman · Publisher: Burak Güngör

This Privacy Policy describes how Dynasty: Ottoman (the "App"), published by Burak Güngör, handles information when you use it.

Summary

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data about you. The App is a single-player game. There are no accounts, no login, no analytics SDK, and no advertising. Game progress is saved locally on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, may also be backed up to your own iCloud account so it can be restored after reinstalling the App or switching devices. We never receive or have access to this iCloud data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contact information
  • Your device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV) for advertising or tracking
  • Your location
  • Your photos, contacts, calendar, microphone, or camera
  • Your gameplay statistics for analytics purposes
  • Crash reports beyond Apple's standard system-level diagnostics (which you can disable in iOS Settings)

2. Local Data and iCloud Backup

The App stores your game progress, settings, and purchase status in a sandboxed file on your device. Uninstalling the App removes this local data.

In addition, Dynasty: Ottoman may use Apple iCloud to back up your game progress so it can be restored after reinstalling the App or moving to another device. This backup uses Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage tied to your own Apple ID; it is stored in your personal iCloud account, which we cannot access. This data is used only for app functionality (restoring your progress) and is never used for tracking, advertising, or any other purpose. If you are not signed in to iCloud, the App simply uses local save and no backup is made.

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock the full game ("Premium Unlock"). This purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, see, or store your payment information at any point. Apple's privacy policy governs the payment transaction: apple.com/legal/privacy.

To verify whether you have made the purchase, the App uses Apple's StoreKit framework, which provides a yes/no response. We do not transmit this information to any server.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs (analytics, advertising, social, crash reporting, or otherwise). The only systems the App communicates with are Apple's own services: StoreKit for the in-app purchase, and iCloud for the optional game-progress backup described in Section 2. Both are operated by Apple, not by us.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is rated for users aged 13 and over (App Store Age Rating: 13+) and is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no personal data from anyone — adult or child — the App is compliant with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the European GDPR-K by design. If you become aware that a child under 13 is using the App without your knowledge, there is no account or stored record to delete, as we retain no data.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Effective date" at the top and publish the new version at the same URL. Continued use of the App after a change indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, write to 9er.game@gmail.com.
